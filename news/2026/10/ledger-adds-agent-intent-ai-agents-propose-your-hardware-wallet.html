Ledger adds Agent Intent: AI agents propose, your hardware wallet approves

Oct 15, 2026 · Source: X

Ledger introduced Agent Intent at its Paris event: AI agents can propose actions such as payments, but each one has to be approved on the user's hardware wallet. Private keys stay with the user, and the beta is free on compatible devices.
