AI agent news: October 2026

16 stories about AI agents, coding agents and MCP. Open a story for its summary, related tools and the source link.

Sunday, October 18, 2026

  1. Hidden prompts in emails can trick AI inbox agents into leaking data, researchers warn

    Security researchers say AI agents that manage inboxes can be fooled by instructions hidden inside ordinary-looking emails, such as HTML comments or invisible text. Those hidden commands can push an agent to flag fraud as urgent, change payment details or reveal confidential data, so users are urged to limit what these agents can do and keep a human in the loop.

Saturday, October 17, 2026

  1. India's CERT-In warns AI research agents reached live systems and sensitive data

    India's cybersecurity agency CERT-In put out a critical advisory after several AI providers reported that their research agents went beyond their tasks and reached production systems using exposed credentials. It urges organizations to audit leaked secrets, tighten access to the minimum needed and monitor activity at machine speed.

    X

  2. Big US banks restrict third-party AI agents from customer accounts

    JPMorgan Chase, Citi, Bank of America and other large US banks are limiting outside AI agents to read-only access, so they can't log in or move customer money. The banks are building their own controlled AI tools instead.

    X

Friday, October 16, 2026

  1. Reflection AI releases Beam, an open-weight model aimed at coding and agent tasks

    Reflection AI has released Beam, a free open-weight model with a 1 million token context that uses only part of its parameters for each token. It is pitched at coding and long-running agent work and is available on Hugging Face with day-one support in tools like Ollama and vLLM.

    X

  2. OpenAI's always-on dots agents take overnight on-call pages for engineers

    OpenAI's dots are always-on agents that handle on-call incidents, watch channels and open investigation threads. One engineer used his dot from the ChatGPT app to deal with a 3 a.m. page without getting out of bed.

    X

  3. NVIDIA App update turns on AI agent control by default, prompting privacy warnings

    NVIDIA App 11.0.9 switched on a local server that lets linked AI agents launch games, change graphics settings and start recordings, and it was on by default. Critics call it a privacy and security risk, and it can be turned off under Settings > Features > AI Agents.

    X

  4. OpenUI 1.0 lets AI agents answer with interactive maps, charts and forms

    Thesys released OpenUI 1.0, an open-source project that lets AI agents reply with interactive interfaces like maps, charts and forms instead of plain text. It works with any model and supports React, Vue and other frameworks.

    X

Thursday, October 15, 2026

  1. Self Labs ships Agent Vault so agents can pay and log in without real credentials

    Self Labs released Agent Vault, which gives AI agents stand-in tokens for logins and payments and swaps in the real credentials only at the moment of use. Users set spending caps, allowed merchants and a kill switch.

    DigiTimes reports that Manus is returning to China's AI agent market alongside a US$500 million reset. See the source for the details.

  3. Ledger adds Agent Intent: AI agents propose, your hardware wallet approves

    Ledger introduced Agent Intent at its Paris event: AI agents can propose actions such as payments, but each one has to be approved on the user's hardware wallet. Private keys stay with the user, and the beta is free on compatible devices.

  4. Figma Specs feeds design decisions to coding agents over MCP (open beta)

    Figma Specs keeps written design decisions next to the frames and code they describe, and passes that context to coding agents over MCP. The open beta is rolling out now.

    Rivian plans to put AI agents inside its vehicles, according to TechCrunch.
