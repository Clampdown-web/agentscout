Jev

Newpersonal agentsOpen source

Jev is an open-source chat assistant for Android, Windows and macOS that reads the conversation on your screen, judges what the other person wants and whether a message looks risky, and drafts candidate replies for you. It fills in the reply box, but you always decide whether to send, so it helps with tricky messages without acting on its own.

Added Oct 23, 2026

Claude Duet

Newpersonal agentsPaid

Claude Duet is Anthropic's proactive personal agent app, in research preview. You message it like an assistant: it sends a morning brief from your connected apps, keeps watch on your inbox, calendar and follow-ups through the day, and only pings you when something needs you. You can also hand it tasks, from scheduling meetings to building dashboards or simple web apps, and it keeps working when you step away but checks with you before acting on your behalf. It runs on iOS, Android and claude.ai, starting with a waitlist for US Pro and Max subscribers on their existing plan limits.

Added Oct 22, 2026

Teebots

Newpersonal agentsFree

Teebots is a free desktop app that keeps a small team of AI bots on your own computer. Each bot can use a different model through your own API key, with requests going straight to the provider, and keeps its own private memory alongside a shared group memory. Put several bots in one group chat and @mention one to hand it a task, or let them pass steps to each other. Bots can work with files in their own folder, run commands, use skills including MCP and browse in their own browser, but they stop and wait for your approval before submitting or paying for anything. Scheduled daily or weekly jobs keep running from the menu bar. Chats, memory, files and logins stay local. Available for Mac (Apple silicon) now, with Windows and Linux coming soon.

Added Oct 22, 2026

Hermex

Newpersonal agentsOpen source

Hermex is a native iPhone and iPad app for controlling a self-hosted Hermes agent. The agent keeps running on your own server; the app connects to it over your private Tailscale network or an HTTPS tunnel, streams replies, lets you answer approval requests, manage tasks and Kanban boards, browse the agent's workspace and get push notifications when it needs you. It is free on the App Store with no in-app purchases, and its source is open.

Added Oct 21, 2026

LVLZ

Newpersonal agentsPaid

LVLZ is a personal AI agent you run by messaging it like a person, on iMessage or WhatsApp, as a text, voice note or call. It runs errands on real websites in a cloud browser, such as bookings, forms and shopping, turns messages into tasks and projects, fills a CRM from your conversations, and runs routines like a morning brief or a price watch. Everything it does lands in a web workspace, its memory can be read, edited and exported, and it asks before paying, contacting someone new or deleting anything, with cards and passwords kept in an encrypted vault.

Added Oct 21, 2026

Sundae

Newpersonal agentsFreemium

Sundae is a personal AI assistant you text from iMessage or Android RCS, with no app or account needed. It works on a computer of its own: it books tables, finds flights at current prices, compares shops, reads letters and photos you send, sets reminders, watches prices and builds simple web pages, then texts back with a screenshot so you can check. It remembers what you tell it, asks before spending money, sending, deleting or opening accounts, and stops at checkout for you to pay. It is free to start with a daily limit, with a paid Plus plan, and is in limited beta.
