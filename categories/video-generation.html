iArt is an AI motion graphics agent that turns a text prompt, or uploaded images, PDFs, slide decks, audio or a web link, into animated scenes. The free edition on GitHub is for noncommercial use; onetake Pro adds a camera operator that follows each beat, source files for every example film, starter templates and a commercial licence.
