Career Ops alternatives (2026)

Career Ops is a personal agents tool: Open-source job search agent that scans job boards and scores each posting against your CV before you apply. Pricing, as the maker states it: Open source. Its GitHub repo has 75,234 stars. We found 72 tools in the directory that do overlapping work (personal assistance, research, open-source agents); 41 of them are open source and 10 list a free or freemium plan. Below are the 10 closest, ranked by how many tasks and sub-tasks they share with Career Ops, then by GitHub stars.
