Novu alternatives (2026)

Novu is a MCP & infra tool: Open-source communication infrastructure that gives products and AI agents one API to reach users over in-app inbox, email, SMS, push, Slack, Teams and chat. Pricing, as the maker states it: See site. Its GitHub repo has 40,164 stars. We found 267 tools in the directory that do overlapping work (writing and managing email, connecting tools with MCP); 149 of them are open source and 24 list a free or freemium plan.
