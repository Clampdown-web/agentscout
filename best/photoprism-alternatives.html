PhotoPrism is a photography & photo editing tool: Self-hosted AI photo library that auto-tags, recognizes faces and finds places. Pricing, as the maker states it: Community edition free (AGPL-3.0, self-hosted); Essentials €2/mo, Plus from €6/mo; managed hosting via PikaPods from $6.50/mo. Its GitHub repo has 40,377 stars. We found 8 tools in the directory that do overlapping work (organizing photo libraries); 2 of them are open source and 2 list a free or freemium plan. Below are the 8 closest, ranked by how many tasks and sub-tasks they share with PhotoPrism, then by GitHub stars.

Updated November 5, 2026

  1. Immich (Open source): Self-hosted photo and video library with AI face recognition and smart search, a private alternative to Google Photos. Consider it because it also covers video; it has more GitHub stars (117,678 vs 40,377).
  2. Mylio Photos (Freemium): Private photo library across your devices with on-device AI tagging and face recognition. Consider it because it does the same job with a different approach (freemium).
  3. Upscayl (Open source): Free open-source desktop app for upscaling images on your own computer. Consider it because it also covers computer and desktop control and color and upscaling; it has more GitHub stars (51,327 vs 40,377).
  4. AI Haircut (Paid): AI Haircut lets you try hairstyles and hair colors on your own photo across 160+ styles before you commit. Consider it because it is a managed commercial product, if you'd rather not run it yourself.
  5. EnhanceWizard (Freemium): EnhanceWizard is a browser-based AI image enhancer and upscaler that sharpens, unblurs, denoises and restores photos at original size, 2x or 4x. Its free browser tier processes images locally on your device up to 16 MP (8,192 px per side) with no sign-up, daily limit or watermark; optional cloud credits unlock Standard HD batch jobs up to 10 images and AI Retouch. Consider it because it also covers browser automation and color and upscaling.
  6. Excire (Paid): AI photo search, keywording and culling as a Lightroom plug-in or standalone app. Consider it because it is a managed commercial product, if you'd rather not run it yourself.
  7. ON1 Photo RAW (Paid): Raw photo editor with AI masking, noise reduction and keywording. Consider it because it is a managed commercial product, if you'd rather not run it yourself; it also covers IDE and editor agents and editing and rough cuts.
  8. Peakto (Paid): AI media organizer for Mac that catalogs photos across Lightroom, Capture One and Apple Photos. Consider it because it is a managed commercial product, if you'd rather not run it yourself.
