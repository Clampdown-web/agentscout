Upscayl alternatives (2026)

Upscayl is a photography & photo editing tool: Free open-source desktop app for upscaling images on your own computer. Pricing, as the maker states it: Free and open source (AGPL-3.0) desktop app; Upscayl Cloud Pro $24.99/mo. Its GitHub repo has 50,719 stars. We found 24 tools in the directory that do overlapping work (open-source image generation, upscaling and restoring photos); 2 of them are open source and 14 list a free or freemium plan. Below are the 10 closest, ranked by how many tasks and sub-tasks they share with Upscayl, then by GitHub stars.

  1. CClipdrop (Freemium): Image cleanup, relighting, background removal and upscaling tools (owned by Jasper). Consider it because it also covers VFX and compositing.
  2. TTopaz Gigapixel (Paid): AI image upscaler that enlarges photos while recovering detail and faces. Consider it because it is a managed commercial product, if you'd rather not run it yourself.
  3. FFotor (Freemium): Online photo editor and design tool with AI enhance, background removal and generation. Consider it because it also covers IDE and editor agents and editing and rough cuts.
  4. PPixelmator Pro (Paid): Apple's Mac image editor with machine-learning enhance, denoise and background removal. Consider it because it is a managed commercial product, if you'd rather not run it yourself; it also covers IDE and editor agents and editing and rough cuts.
  5. CClaid.ai (Freemium): AI product photography: backgrounds, fashion models and upscaling, with an API. Consider it because it does the same job with a different approach (freemium).
  6. LLet's Enhance (Freemium): Online upscaling and enhancement for photos and print. Consider it because it does the same job with a different approach (freemium).
  7. MMagnific (Freemium): Creative upscaler and relighter that grew into Freepik's full AI suite (Freepik's AI tools now live under Magnific). Consider it because it also covers VFX and compositing.
  8. PPicWish (Freemium): Background remover, photo enhancer and AI editing tools for web and mobile, with an API. Consider it because it also covers editing and rough cuts and mobile and remote control.
  9. RRemini (Freemium): Photo enhancer that sharpens blurry or old photos and restores faces. Consider it because it does the same job with a different approach (freemium).
  10. IImmich (Open source): Self-hosted photo and video library with AI face recognition and smart search, a private alternative to Google Photos. Consider it because it also covers agent frameworks and SDKs and video; it has more GitHub stars (116,585 vs 50,719).
