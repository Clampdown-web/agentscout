Upscayl alternatives (2026)

Upscayl is a photography & photo editing tool: Free open-source desktop app for upscaling images on your own computer. Pricing, as the maker states it: Free and open source (AGPL-3.0) desktop app; Upscayl Cloud free trial (10 credits), Pro $24.99/mo. Its GitHub repo has 50,991 stars. We found 28 tools in the directory that do overlapping work (open-source image generation, upscaling and restoring photos); 2 of them are open source and 18 list a free or freemium plan. Below are the 10 closest, ranked by how many tasks and sub-tasks they share with Upscayl, then by GitHub stars.

  1. EnhanceWizard (Freemium): EnhanceWizard is a browser-based AI image enhancer and upscaler that sharpens, unblurs, denoises and restores photos at original size, 2x or 4x. Its free browser tier processes images locally on your device up to 16 MP (8,192 px per side) with no sign-up, daily limit or watermark; optional cloud credits unlock Standard HD batch jobs up to 10 images and AI Retouch. Consider it because it also covers browser automation and dialogue cleanup and repair.
  2. Clipdrop (Freemium): Image cleanup, relighting, background removal and upscaling tools (owned by Jasper). Consider it because it also covers VFX and compositing.
  3. Editara (Freemium): Editara is a browser-based AI photo editor where you upload an image and describe what to change in plain English—removing objects or people, replacing or removing backgrounds, restoring and colorizing old photos, extending frames and upscaling—across eight selectable models including GPT Image 2, Nano Banana Pro and Seedream 5 Pro, with 12 free daily credits and watermark-free exports. Consider it because it does the same job with a different approach (freemium).
  4. Images Upscale (Freemium): Images Upscale is a browser-based AI image upscaler that uses cloud Real-ESRGAN models to enlarge JPG, PNG and WebP files 2x or 4x with modes tuned for photos, anime and illustrations, product shots and screenshots. It supports multi-image and ZIP batch uploads, watermark-free downloads, and companion tools for background removal, unblurring and alt-text generation. Consider it because it also covers browser automation.
  5. Topaz Gigapixel (Paid): AI image upscaler that enlarges photos while recovering detail and faces. Consider it because it is a managed commercial product, if you'd rather not run it yourself.
  6. Fotor (Freemium): Online photo editor and design tool with AI enhance, background removal and generation. Consider it because it also covers IDE and editor agents and editing and rough cuts.
  7. Pixelmator Pro (Paid): Apple's Mac image editor with machine-learning enhance, denoise and background removal. Consider it because it is a managed commercial product, if you'd rather not run it yourself; it also covers IDE and editor agents and editing and rough cuts.
  8. Claid.ai (Freemium): AI product photography: backgrounds, fashion models and upscaling, with an API. Consider it because it does the same job with a different approach (freemium).
  9. Let's Enhance (Freemium): Online upscaling and enhancement for photos and print. Consider it because it does the same job with a different approach (freemium).
  10. Magnific (Freemium): Creative upscaler and relighter that grew into Freepik's full AI suite (Freepik's AI tools now live under Magnific). Consider it because it also covers VFX and compositing.
