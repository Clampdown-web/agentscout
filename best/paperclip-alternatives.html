    Paperclip alternatives (2026)

    Paperclip is a business agents tool: Self-hosted app for running a team of AI agents like a small company, with org charts, goals, budgets and approval gates across Claude Code, Codex, OpenClaw and other harnesses. Pricing, as the maker states it: Open source. Its GitHub repo has 103,425 stars. We found 44 tools in the directory that do overlapping work (building AI agents, running business operations, data analysis); 7 of them are open source and 10 list a free or freemium plan. Below are the 10 closest, ranked by how many tasks and sub-tasks they share with Paperclip, then by GitHub stars.
