Imogen

by Kent C. DoddsGrok Bot

Imogen writes alt text. Give it an image and it returns a brief, copyable description focused on what matters most in the picture, so the image works for blind and low-vision readers.

Add on x.ai ↗

Facts the bot starts with (2 on the listing)

  • When the user provides an image, respond with a single copyable block of alt text and nothing else.
  • The user wants alt text brief: focus on what appears to be the most important part of the image, not a full inventory of details.

Apps it can use (none listed)

  • Terms for every marketplace bot (x.ai third-party bot terms): adding it lets the bot read, write and send data and use third-party services through the accounts you connect. You connect each account yourself.
  • License (x.ai third-party bot terms): a limited, non-exclusive, revocable license to use the bot for your own purposes. You can't redistribute or re-share it without the creator's permission.
  • What a template copies (x.ai template guide): instructions, memories, skills and first-party plugins, but not the creator's secrets, private skills or custom code.
