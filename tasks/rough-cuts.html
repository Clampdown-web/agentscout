    Best AI tools for rough cuts and assembly edits (2026)

    Assistant editors that log footage, pick selects, remove bad takes and silences and assemble a first cut you can finish in your editor. We list 10 tools for rough cuts and assembly edits below, each with a link-checked page, pricing as the maker states it and alternatives.

    Updated October 18, 2026

    10 tools: 4 free or freemium, 5 paid, usage-based or enterprise, 1 pricing on the maker's site.

    Adobe Premiere AI Assistant

    Newfilm & TV productionPaid

    Chat panel inside Adobe Premiere (public beta). Ask it in plain words to sort footage into bins, transcribe, find slates, add markers and build stringouts or rough assemblies. Every step can be undone.

    Added Oct 18, 2026

    AutoPod

    Newfilm & TV productionPaid

    Premiere Pro plugin for automatic multicam editing, jump cuts and social exports of podcasts and talk shows.

    Added Oct 18, 2026

    Cleanvoice

    Newfilm & TV audioFreemium

    AI that removes filler words, mouth sounds, silences and background noise from speech recordings.

    Added Oct 18, 2026

    Cutback

    Newfilm & TV productionPaid

    Premiere Pro assistant that auto-edits raw footage and multicam: picks selects, cuts silences, transcribes and translates.

    Added Oct 18, 2026

    Descript

    Newfilm & TV productionFreemium

    Video and audio editor you edit like a doc. Its Underlord co-editor can cut filler words, build rough cuts, add captions, clean up audio and write show notes when you ask it to.

    Added Oct 18, 2026

    Eddie AI

    Newfilm & TV productionUsage-based

    AI assistant editor for interviews and documentary footage. Chat with it, or connect it to ChatGPT or Claude over MCP, to get rough cuts you can export to Premiere, Resolve or Final Cut.

    Added Oct 18, 2026

    Gling

    Newfilm & TV productionFreemium

    AI video editor for YouTubers. Removes bad takes, silences and filler words and exports to your editor.

    Added Oct 18, 2026

    OpusClip

    Newfilm & TV productionFreemium

    Turns long videos into short clips with AI: picks highlights, reframes, adds captions and scores clips.

    Added Oct 18, 2026

    Quickture

    Newfilm & TV productionSee site

    AI editing assistant for pro editors that works inside Avid Media Composer and Premiere. Its chat agent can organize bins, find key shots and assemble rough cuts and stringouts.

    Added Oct 18, 2026

    Trint

    Newfilm & TV productionPaid

    AI transcription and production workflow for newsrooms and producers: transcribe, caption, translate and build rough cuts from text.
