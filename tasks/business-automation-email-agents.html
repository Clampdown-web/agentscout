Assistants that draft replies and manage your inbox. Of the 213 tools on our running business operations page, 10 fit this narrower job. Daily Stock Analysis

Newfinance & accountingOpen source

Daily Stock Analysis is an MIT-licensed, LLM-driven system that analyses a personal watchlist across A-shares, Hong Kong, US, Japanese, Korean and Taiwanese stocks and ETFs, pulling quotes, technical indicators, news and filings, then pushes a daily decision dashboard with scores, levels, risk alerts and a checklist to WeCom, Feishu, Telegram, Discord, Slack or email. It runs free on GitHub Actions, Docker or locally, has a web and desktop workbench with backtests, and an agent mode for follow-up questions using 15 built-in strategies. It is not financial advice.
