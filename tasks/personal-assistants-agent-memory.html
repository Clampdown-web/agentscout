Sundae

Newpersonal agentsFreemium

Sundae is a personal AI assistant you text from iMessage or Android RCS, with no app or account needed. It works on a computer of its own: it books tables, finds flights at current prices, compares shops, reads letters and photos you send, sets reminders, watches prices and builds simple web pages, then texts back with a screenshot so you can check. It remembers what you tell it, asks before spending money, sending, deleting or opening accounts, and stops at checkout for you to pay. It is free to start with a daily limit, with a paid Plus plan, and is in limited beta.

Teebots

Newpersonal agentsFree

Teebots is a free desktop app that keeps a small team of AI bots on your own computer. Each bot can use a different model through your own API key, with requests going straight to the provider, and keeps its own private memory alongside a shared group memory. Put several bots in one group chat and @mention one to hand it a task, or let them pass steps to each other. Bots can work with files in their own folder, run commands, use skills including MCP and browse in their own browser, but they stop and wait for your approval before submitting or paying for anything. Scheduled daily or weekly jobs keep running from the menu bar. Chats, memory, files and logins stay local. Available for Mac (Apple silicon) now, with Windows and Linux coming soon.
