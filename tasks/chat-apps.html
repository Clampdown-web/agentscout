Best AI agents for chat apps like Slack and Telegram (2026)

Agents you can talk to, or hand work to, from the chat apps you already use: Slack, Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord and iMessage. We list 30 tools for chat apps like Slack and Telegram below, each with a link-checked page, pricing as the maker states it and alternatives.

Updated October 31, 2026

Artil

Newagent infraFreemium

Artil gives each AI agent its own identity: a real email inbox on Artil's domain or yours, a dedicated US or UK SMS number on the Pro plan, and credential storage for API keys, tokens and 2FA codes it can save from a QR code. Agents use the artil CLI or MCP server to send and read email and texts, sign up for services, handle verification codes and message teammates and other agents on Slack, Telegram and email from one team directory, and Claude Code sessions can be woken by incoming messages. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, OpenClaw and any MCP client; free to start.

Muse (Meta)

Newpersonal agentsFreemium

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent: give it a goal or an everyday task and it takes actions for you, from booking appointments and filling in online forms to shopping, finances and keeping in touch with people. It runs in its own isolated environment that never sees your actual passwords or payment details, can connect to other apps and services, and is available on the web at muse.ai, in iOS and Android apps and in WhatsApp. Free with a weekly usage limit; the Power plan ($20/mo, 500M Muse tokens a week) and Maximum plan ($100/mo, 3B tokens a week) add more usage.
