Agents in chat apps tools for personal AI assistants

Agents you can reach from Slack, Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord or iMessage. Of the 125 tools on our personal assistance page, 12 fit this narrower job. By pricing: 6 open source, 4 free or freemium, 2 paid, usage-based or enterprise. The most-starred open repos here are OpenClaw (393,429 stars), NanoClaw (31,057 stars), OpenWorker (18,751 stars). Most come from the personal agents (12) category.

Updated November 9, 2026

OpenClaw

personal agentsOpen source

OpenClaw is an MIT-licensed personal AI assistant that runs on your own computer and answers in the chat apps you already use, including Discord, iMessage, Slack, Teams, Telegram and WhatsApp, with native apps for macOS, iOS, Android, Windows and Linux. State, memory and credentials stay on your hardware, models and harnesses such as Claude, Codex or local models are swappable plugins, and one gateway can run a personal or shared multiplayer assistant. It is stewarded by the non-profit OpenClaw Foundation with no paid tier or hosted service.

LVLZ

personal agentsPaid

LVLZ is a personal AI agent you run by messaging it like a person, on iMessage or WhatsApp, as a text, voice note or call. It runs errands on real websites in a cloud browser, such as bookings, forms and shopping, turns messages into tasks and projects, fills a CRM from your conversations, and runs routines like a morning brief or a price watch. Everything it does lands in a web workspace, its memory can be read, edited and exported, and it asks before paying, contacting someone new or deleting anything, with cards and passwords kept in an encrypted vault.

Added Oct 21, 2026

Muse (Meta)

personal agentsFreemium

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent: give it a goal or an everyday task and it takes actions for you, from booking appointments and filling in online forms to shopping, finances and keeping in touch with people. It runs in its own isolated environment that never sees your actual passwords or payment details, can connect to other apps and services, and is available on the web at muse.ai, in iOS and Android apps and in WhatsApp. Free with a weekly usage limit; the Power plan ($20/mo, 500M Muse tokens a week) and Maximum plan ($100/mo, 3B tokens a week) add more usage.

Added Oct 31, 2026

Sundae

personal agentsFreemium

Sundae is a personal AI assistant you text from iMessage or Android RCS, with no app or account needed. It works on a computer of its own: it books tables, finds flights at current prices, compares shops, reads letters and photos you send, sets reminders, watches prices and builds simple web pages, then texts back with a screenshot so you can check. It remembers what you tell it, asks before spending money, sending, deleting or opening accounts, and stops at checkout for you to pay. It is free to start with a daily limit, with a paid Plus plan, and is in limited beta.
