    UseIt Studio

    Newgraphic design & brandingFreemium

    UseIt Studio is an AI design agent for things you can hold. You describe what you want in a sentence, it asks the questions a designer would, and you refine any detail by pointing at it. Instead of a flat mockup you get a real, editable design file ready to print, cut and fold, with true dimensions, bleed and dielines, for pins, stickers, gift boxes, pop-up cards, packaging and brand kits. It runs in the browser, and a Windows desktop helper lets you call it up anywhere.
