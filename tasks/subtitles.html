Best AI subtitle and caption tools (2026)

Transcription, captioning and translated subtitles for film, TV and online video, from editor plugins to live broadcast captions. We list 25 tools for subtitles and captions below, each with a link-checked page, pricing as the maker states it and alternatives.

Updated November 8, 2026

25 tools: 2 open source, 12 free or freemium, 9 paid, usage-based or enterprise, 2 pricing on the maker's site.

ChatCut

Newvideo generationFreemium

ChatCut is an AI video editor for agents: its MCP server and plugins let Codex, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Cursor and others edit your footage, add captions, remove silences and filler words, create motion graphics, generate voiceovers and music and export an editable timeline.

Added Nov 8, 2026

Audio AI Studio

Newfilm & TV audioFreemium

Concat

film & TV productionOpen source

Concat is a free, open-source video editor meant as a CapCut replacement that runs entirely on your own machine. It covers auto-captions, text-to-speech voices, background removal, keyframes, titles, effects, filters and transitions with multi-track editing up to 4K, with no watermark, account or subscription. It runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS, and it can be scripted through a documented JSON-RPC and gRPC API, so scripts and AI agents can drive edits.

Added Oct 21, 2026

video generationFreemium

You can edit any scene by asking in plain language, clone your own voice, cast AI characters, and on Pro use its API and MCP access for Claude and ChatGPT.

iArt

video generationFreemium

iArt is an AI motion graphics agent that turns a text prompt, or uploaded images, PDFs, slide decks, audio or a web link, into animated scenes. It handles explainers, logo animations, kinetic typography, animated charts, lower thirds and audio-synced captions, adds AI voiceover or a cloned voice, and exports MP4 (1080p on Pro, 4K on Ultra) or transparent WebM overlays.
