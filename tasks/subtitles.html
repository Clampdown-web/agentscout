Best AI subtitle and caption tools (2026)

Transcription, captioning and translated subtitles for film, TV and online video, from editor plugins to live broadcast captions. We list 23 tools for subtitles and captions below, each with a link-checked page, pricing as the maker states it and alternatives.

Updated October 23, 2026

23 tools: 2 open source, 10 free or freemium, 9 paid, usage-based or enterprise, 2 pricing on the maker's site.

CutBay

Newvideo generationSee site

CutBay is a chat-based AI video agent that turns ideas, web links, documents, slides, spreadsheets and your own media into finished videos. You describe what you want and it drafts explainers, product demos, slideshows, animated charts, talking-head clips or YouTube Shorts, then lets you keep editing by chat, with add-ons such as B-roll and automatic subtitles.

Added Oct 23, 2026

Vidmoat

Newvideo generationFreemium

Vidmoat is a hosted AI video editor you direct in chat. Describe the edit and its agent cuts dead air, adds word-synced captions, grades and reframes your footage on a real timeline, where you can review and change any cut by hand before rendering an MP4. It works in the browser, on Telegram (@vidmoat_bot) and over MCP (https://api.vidmoat.com/api/mcp, OAuth), so Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor can drive edits too.

Added Oct 23, 2026

Concat

Newfilm & TV productionOpen source

Concat is a free, open-source video editor meant as a CapCut replacement that runs entirely on your own machine. It covers auto-captions, text-to-speech voices, background removal, keyframes, titles, effects, filters and transitions with multi-track editing up to 4K, with no watermark, account or subscription. It runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS, and it can be scripted through a documented JSON-RPC and gRPC API, so scripts and AI agents can drive edits.

Added Oct 21, 2026

ClipTalk

Newvideo generationFreemium

ClipTalk is an AI video agent that makes finished short-form videos for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Give it a prompt, script, product, website, photo or link and it writes the script, generates the visuals, adds a voiceover from 140+ AI voices in 30+ languages, word-by-word captions, B-roll and music, then renders a ready-to-post MP4. You can edit any scene by asking in plain language, clone your own voice, cast AI characters, and on Pro use its API and MCP access for Claude and ChatGPT.

Added Oct 21, 2026

OpenChatCut

Newfilm & TV productionOpen source + cloud

A local-first AI video editor with a multi-track timeline that you drive by chatting with an agent; the AGPL edition is free, and a Pro desktop app adds transcription-based editing.

Added Oct 20, 2026

BaoCut

Newfilm & TV productionSee site

An AI video agent for macOS and Windows that transcribes, subtitles, translates, dubs, cuts and animates your footage from plain instructions, with every result landing in an editable timeline instead of a one-shot export.

Added Oct 20, 2026

iArt

Newvideo generationFreemium

iArt is an AI motion graphics agent that turns a text prompt, or uploaded images, PDFs, slide decks, audio or a web link, into animated scenes. It writes real animation code scene by scene (layout, typography, timing, transitions), so text stays exactly spelled and you can refine the result by chatting ("make the title two seconds shorter"). It handles explainers, logo animations, kinetic typography, animated charts, lower thirds and audio-synced captions, adds AI voiceover or a cloned voice, and exports MP4 (1080p on Pro, 4K on Ultra) or transparent WebM overlays.
