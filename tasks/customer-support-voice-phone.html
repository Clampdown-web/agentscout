Voice and phone agents for customer support

Platforms for agents that listen and talk: phone lines, real-time speech and voice assistants. Of the 353 tools on our customer support page, 46 fit this narrower job. By pricing: 5 open source, 8 free or freemium, 13 paid, usage-based or enterprise, 20 pricing on the maker's site. The most-starred open repos here are Fish Audio (33,488 stars), Tel-Agent (1,170 stars), Bolna (809 stars). Most come from the MCP servers (20), voice (9), customer support (7) categories.

Fish Audio

voiceFreemium

Fish Audio is a voice AI platform for text to speech, voice cloning and speech to text, with a real-time API for voice agents. Its Drama 3 and S2 models take inline emotion and delivery tags such as whispering, laughing or pauses, it can clone a voice from a few seconds of audio, and its library has more than two million community voices for narration, characters, audiobooks and support agents. The Fish Speech models behind it are published on GitHub and Hugging Face.
