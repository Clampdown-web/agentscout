Fish Audio

voiceFreemium

Fish Audio is a voice AI platform for text to speech, voice cloning and speech to text, with a real-time API for voice agents. Its Drama 3 and S2 models take inline emotion and delivery tags such as whispering, laughing or pauses, it can clone a voice from a few seconds of audio, and its library has more than two million community voices for narration, characters, audiobooks and support agents. The Fish Speech models behind it are published on GitHub and Hugging Face.

DIRI AI

DIRI AI is a voice role-play trainer for salespeople. You pick a situation, such as a price objection, an intro meeting, a cold call or a client who wants to leave, set the difficulty, and talk out loud with an AI customer that reacts to what you actually say rather than a fixed script. After each call you get an instant report with a transcript, the key moments highlighted and specific stronger wording to try next time, plus a history of your practice. Managers can use it to help new hires ramp up before real calls.

Mercury 3

models & local runtimesUsage-based

Mercury 3 is Inception's diffusion language model, which writes many tokens in parallel rather than one at a time, reaching over 1,000 tokens per second on NVIDIA GPUs with a 1M-token context, tool calling and structured outputs. It has an OpenAI-compatible API and is also offered on OpenRouter, Baseten and Vercel, which makes it a fit for voice bots, support agents and browser agents where response speed matters. Pricing is per million input and output tokens.
