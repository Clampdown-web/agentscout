No-code app builders for workflow automation

Builders that turn a description or a drag-and-drop canvas into a working app or agent, without writing code. Of the 241 tools on our automating workflows page, 21 fit this narrower job. By pricing: 3 open source, 10 free or freemium, 6 paid, usage-based or enterprise, 2 pricing on the maker's site. The most-starred open repos here are Langflow (156,004 stars), Flowise (55,525 stars), Kestra (29,990 stars). Most come from the no-code builders (10), app builders (5), agent infra (1) categories.

Kestra

Newagent infraOpen source + enterprise

Kestra is an open-source, event-driven orchestration and scheduling platform where workflows are declared in YAML or built in a no-code editor. It includes an AI Agent task that gives flows an LLM with memory and tools, supports nested agents and the Agent-to-Agent (A2A) protocol, and can expose any flow as a tool to external AI agents through its MCP server. It runs in the cloud, on-prem or air-gapped and is Apache 2.0 licensed.
