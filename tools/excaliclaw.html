About ExcaliClaw

ExcaliClaw is listed in our productivity category and is aimed at people who want a ready-to-use product. Hand-drawn diagram workspace your agent can draw in via MCP (Claude, Cursor, VS Code). Pricing, as the maker states it: Free plan; paid from EUR 9/mo. Maturity: Launched Oct 12. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-14.

What is ExcaliClaw?

ExcaliClaw is a collaborative workspace for hand-drawn diagrams where you organize, search, share and create diagrams with Claude, Cursor and VS Code through MCP.

It is an independent workspace.

Sources: the official site and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: excaliclaw.com.

Who is ExcaliClaw for?

People who diagram with AI assistants.

ExcaliClaw pricing

  • Free: $0/mo · solo use
  • Starter: $10.11/mo · teams up to 5
  • Pro: $32.59/mo · teams up to 20
  • Enterprise: Custom · SSO and a contract

Extra members beyond a plan's seats are $3.37 a month.
