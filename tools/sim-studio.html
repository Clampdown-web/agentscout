About Sim Studio

Sim Studio is listed in our no-code builders category and is aimed at builders. Workspace for visually building, deploying and monitoring AI agents and workflows. Pricing, as the maker states it: Open source + cloud. Maturity: Mature. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-16.

What is Sim Studio?

Sim is a workspace to build, deploy and manage AI agents and workflows.

It connects 270+ integrations and has a desktop app for macOS 12+ that can point at a self-hosted deployment.
