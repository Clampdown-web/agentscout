    About Elicit

    Elicit is listed in our research category and is aimed at people who want a ready-to-use product. Research assistant that finds and summarizes academic papers. Pricing, as the maker states it: Free tier + paid plans. Maturity: Mature. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-14.

    What is Elicit?

    Elicit is an AI research assistant that helps you synthesize evidence, understand a field, design programs and experiments, draft proposals and weigh tradeoffs.

    Elicit says it is used by over 7 million researchers.

    Sources: the official site and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: elicit.com.

    Researchers who need to find and summarize academic papers.

    Elicit pricing

    • Free: $0 · limited Research Agent and Research Reports usage
    • Pro: $49/user/mo · billed annually ($588 a year)
    • Team: $169/user/mo · billed annually ($2,028 a year), 5x usage, collaboration, admin panel
    • Enterprise: Custom
