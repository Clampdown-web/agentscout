About Nango

Nango is listed in our agent infra category and is aimed at builders. Integration platform that connects products and AI agents to 1,000+ APIs with managed auth, syncs and tool calls. Pricing, as the maker states it: Free plan (hard-capped); Pay-as-you-go $50/mo incl. $50 credits; Enterprise custom; source-available (Elastic License 2.0). Maturity: Mature. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-20.

What is Nango?

Nango is a platform for building product integrations that connects your app and your AI agents to 1,000+ APIs. It gives you three building blocks: managed auth (OAuth, API keys and token refresh, with an embeddable white-label auth flow), a proxy that makes authenticated requests for your users with retries and rate-limit handling, and functions, TypeScript integration logic you write yourself or generate with its AI builder and deploy to Nango's runtime.

Use cases include AI tool calling and MCP, data syncs and webhooks. Nango says it is used in production by Replit, Ramp and Mercor, offers about 7,000 pre-built tools, triggers and syncs, and can be self-hosted on the Enterprise plan; the code is source-available under the Elastic License 2.0.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-20. Official site: nango.dev.

Who is Nango for?

SaaS and AI-agent teams that need their product or agents to act in users' third-party accounts (CRM, email, calendars and more) without building every integration and OAuth flow themselves.
