What is KIWI?

KIWI (app.kiwi) is an AI-native business workspace built for freelancers, agencies and small businesses that combines CRM, email and calendar management, quotes and invoices, project tracking, documents, spreadsheets and a creative studio in one account. Its digital workforce consists of 24 named AI agents—led by Sophia, an always-on Executive Assistant included on the free plan—that read the context of the active page and client to draft replies, prepare quotes, chase invoices and organize tasks.

Every outbound or high-impact action declares its credit cost upfront and waits for explicit human approval before anything is sent, published or deleted, with unattended background runs turned off by default. Before prompts reach underlying language models, KIWI replaces personal names, companies and contact details with pseudonyms and reassembles the final response on its side. Upgrading to Premium ($35/month) unlocks 23 additional specialist agents—including Diego (Sales), Manon (Content), Lena (SEO), Ludovic (Project Manager), Isabella (Financial Analyst) and Kai (Developer)—plus the 14-tool Aukimi Creative Suite, storyboard-to-video studio and multi-network social publishing.

Sources: app.kiwi/agents, app.kiwi/pricing, app.kiwi and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-25.
