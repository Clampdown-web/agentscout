About Antinode Clear

Antinode Clear is listed in our film & TV audio category and is aimed at people who want a ready-to-use product. AI plugin that separates voice, reverb and noise in real time so you can rebalance dialogue (formerly Supertone Clear). Pricing, as the maker states it: One-time purchase; see site. Maturity: Active. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-18.

What is Antinode Clear?

Antinode Clear is a one-time audio plugin for dialogue. On 7 Nov 2026 the maker's site described it as separating voice from background noise and reverb, with de-noise, de-reverb and de-voice in real time, and listed it at $49, shown as $20 off $69.

The AgentScout listing identifies this product as formerly Supertone Clear and keeps the pricing label as a one-time purchase rather than a permanent dollar amount, because the marked-down price can change. The same site also sells other Antinode plugins (Air and Shift); those are separate products and are not this listing.

Sources: antinodeaudio.com and our directory listing, checked 2026-11-07.
