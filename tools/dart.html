What is Dart?

Dart is a project management workspace where people and AI agents work side by side. AI agents can be assigned tasks and execute them, and Dart's AI also plans roadmaps, generates subtasks, fills task properties and writes reports.

It suits startups and teams that want a Linear- or Asana-style tracker with AI built in, and it connects to ChatGPT, Claude and any LLM through its MCP server.

Listed in our productivity category. Official site: dartai.com.

Key features

  • AI agents that take assigned tasks and do the work
  • AI subtask generation, property filling, reporting and duplicate detection
  • List, board, roadmap and calendar layouts with sprints and time tracking
  • MCP server plus ChatGPT and Claude integrations
  • Slack, Discord, GitHub, Notion, Zapier and n8n integrations
  • Desktop apps for Mac, Windows and Linux, plus iOS and Android apps

Dart pricing

Free plan available. Paid plans from $8/month.

  • Personal: $0 · up to 4 teammates, AI agents included
  • Premium: $8 per teammate/mo · unlimited teammates, AI chat and roadmap planning, GitHub and Slack
  • Business: $12 per teammate/mo · SAML SSO and SCIM, analytics, priority support

Paid prices shown are with yearly billing (20% off). Prices as of Oct 2026, from the official pricing page.
