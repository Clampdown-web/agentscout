About Self Agent Vault

Self Agent Vault is listed in our MCP & infra category and is aimed at people who want a ready-to-use product. Payment and login layer for AI agents: agents check out and sign in using mock tokens, while real card numbers and passwords are swapped in out of the agent's view, with spending caps, merchant allowlists and a kill switch. Pricing, as the maker states it: Free, Plus and Enterprise tiers; first 1,000 sign-ups get Plus free for three months. Maturity: Live; launched Oct 15, 2026 by Self Labs. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-16.

What is Self Agent Vault?

Self Agent Vault is a payment and login layer for AI agents. Agents fill checkout and sign-in forms with mock tokens, and real card numbers and passwords are swapped in inside an enclave the agent never sees.

Our listing notes spending caps, merchant allowlists and a kill switch.

Sources: the official site and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: agentvault.io.

People who want their agent to buy things or sign in without exposing real credentials.
