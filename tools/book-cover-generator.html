What is Book Cover Generator?

Book Cover Generator is a story-first AI cover studio built for independent fiction and nonfiction authors. Instead of starting from a blank canvas or writing complex image prompts, you enter your book title, author name, genre (or let the studio infer it) and a short story summary. The studio translates your summary into mood, audience and genre cues and generates two genuinely different visual directions with typography and title hierarchy built into the composition.

Once you compare the two concepts side by side, you can lock the stronger direction and use the built-in editor to adjust the title, subtitle, author placement, color mood or describe a single focused composition change in plain language without losing the rest of the cover. Finished designs export without watermarks in publishing-ready sizes including KDP front cover (6×9 in at 300 DPI PNG), eBook (1600×2560 JPG), square audiobook (3000×3000 JPG), HD (1024×1536 PNG) and social launch cards (1200×630 PNG).
