VoltAgent is listed in our frameworks category and is aimed at builders. TypeScript agent framework plus an engineering platform for building, observing and deploying AI agents. Pricing, as the maker states it: Open source + cloud. Maturity: Mature. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-16.

What is VoltAgent?

VoltAgent is an AI agent engineering platform with two parts: an open-source TypeScript framework (memory, RAG, guardrails, tools, MCP, voice and workflows) and the VoltOps Console for observability, automation, deployment, evals and prompts.

The console is available in the cloud or self-hosted, and the framework connects to any AI provider.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: voltagent.dev.
