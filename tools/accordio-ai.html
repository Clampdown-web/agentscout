About Accordio AI

Accordio AI is listed in our business agents category and is aimed at people who want a ready-to-use product. Adds time tracking, contract signing, invoicing and payments to Claude through a free MCP connector. Pricing, as the maker states it: Freemium. Maturity: Active. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-16.

What is Accordio AI?

Accordio AI adds the tools Claude is missing: track time, sign contracts, send invoices and get paid.

It is a free MCP connector for Claude, Claude Code and any agent, with no trial clock or card required.

Sources: the official site and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: accordio.ai.
