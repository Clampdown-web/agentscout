What is Kwern?

Kwern is a set of AI marketing agents for indie makers. You give it the URL of what you built; one agent per channel drafts posts, replies and ads on your own model key, and nothing publishes until you approve, edit or reject it.

On 23 Nov 2026 the pricing page listed three plans that also decide how many products you run: Lite $29/mo or $290/yr (1 product), Pro $59/mo or $590/yr (3 products) and Max $99/mo or $990/yr (5 products). Who is Kwern for?

Is Kwern free?

Kwern's pricing, as the maker states it: Lite $29/mo or $290/yr (1 product); Pro $59/mo or $590/yr (3 products); Max $99/mo or $990/yr (5 products). Agents run on your own model key, billed by the provider (kwern.app/pricing, 23 Nov 2026).. Check the official site for current plans.

What does Kwern cost?

On 23 Nov 2026 plans were Lite $29/mo or $290/yr, Pro $59/mo or $590/yr, and Max $99/mo or $990/yr, depending on how many products you run.

Do I pay for the model as well?

Yes. The model runs on your own provider key, so token costs are billed by that provider and are not marked up by Kwern.

Does it post on its own?

No. You approve, edit or reject drafts; nothing publishes until you do.
