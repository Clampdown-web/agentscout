      ClipTalk alternatives

      Other video generation tools in our directory. See all video generation tools →

      Hypit

      An open-source agent workflow that takes a viral short video and rebuilds it as an editable template, letting you swap the presenter, script, voice and B-roll to produce many new variants with your own API keys.

      explainroo

      Lets your AI coding agent make narrated explainer videos: it writes the script, builds the visuals and renders the voice and video on your own computer, with no API key needed. Free and open source.

      invideo AI

      Make and edit videos from prompts with its agent, Agent Two, including script, footage, voiceover and edits.

      iArt

      iArt is an AI motion graphics agent that turns a text prompt, or uploaded images, PDFs, slide decks, audio or a web link, into animated scenes. It writes real animation code scene by scene (layout, typography, timing, transitions), so text stays exactly spelled and you can refine the result by chatting ("make the title two seconds shorter"). It handles explainers, logo animations, kinetic typography, animated charts, lower thirds and audio-synced captions, adds AI voiceover or a cloned voice, and exports MP4 (1080p on Pro, 4K on Ultra) or transparent WebM overlays.

      Google Flow

      Google's AI filmmaking tool built on Veo. Generate and extend cinematic shots, keep characters consistent and build scenes, with a Flow agent to help.

      Adobe Firefly

      Adobe's generative app for images, video and audio, with 30+ models including Adobe's commercially safe ones and a creative agent.
