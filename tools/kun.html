What is Kun?

Kun is a desktop app that runs on your computer: code, documents and agent conversations share one local runtime, and sessions, memory and settings stay on your machine. You connect your own model.

Its README (in Chinese) also lists a plugin and skills marketplace (MCP/Skills), reminders and scheduled tasks, a terminal TUI and remote access from your phone; it supports OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible APIs.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: kun-agent.com.
