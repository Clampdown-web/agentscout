About AI Town

AI Town is listed in our games category and is aimed at builders. Deployable starter kit for a virtual town where AI characters live, chat and socialize, inspired by Generative Agents. Pricing, as the maker states it: Open source. Maturity: Mature. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-16.

What is AI Town?

AI Town is a virtual town where AI characters live, chat and socialize.

It is a deployable starter kit for building your own version, inspired by the Generative Agents paper.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: convex.dev/ai-town.
