About LangSmith

LangSmith is listed in our evals & observability category and is aimed at builders. Tracing, evals and monitoring for LLM apps and agents. Pricing, as the maker states it: Free tier + paid plans. Maturity: Mature. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-14.

What is LangSmith?

LangSmith is LangChain's platform for tracing, evaluating and monitoring LLM apps and agents.

The Python client nests every LLM call in a pipeline under a single trace in the LangSmith UI.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: langchain.com/langsmith.

Who is LangSmith for?

Developers who need visibility into LLM apps, with or without LangChain.
