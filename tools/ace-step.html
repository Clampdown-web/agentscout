What is ACE-Step?

ACE-Step is an open-source foundation model for music generation. It combines diffusion-based generation with a deep-compression autoencoder and a lightweight linear transformer to get both speed and long-range musical structure.

The team reports it can create up to four minutes of music in about 20 seconds on an A100 GPU, about 15 times faster than LLM-based baselines, with good lyric alignment. It also supports voice cloning, lyric editing, remixing and track generation such as lyrics to vocals or singing to accompaniment. Weights are on Hugging Face and ModelScope, with a demo Space and a training guide.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-23. Official site: ace-step.github.io.
