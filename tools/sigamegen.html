What is SiGameGen?

SiGameGen is a browser-based AI game generator and community arcade that turns a written game idea into a playable, self-contained HTML5 game in a single step. After the initial build, you can play the game right in the workspace and refine controls, physics, rules, visuals or difficulty through follow-up chat while keeping a full version history of every playable build.

Creators can choose between Fast mode (DeepSeek V4 Flash at 9 credits per generation or edit) for rapid prototyping and Pro mode (Kimi K2.7 Code at 45 credits) for richer mechanics and visuals. Playing games in the public arcade, sharing playable links, opening private two-player room codes, and downloading the standalone HTML file are all free.

Sources: sigamegen.com, sigamegen.com/pricing and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-24.

Indie creators, educators and hobbyists who want to turn game ideas into playable, downloadable single-file HTML5 browser games by chatting with an AI builder.
