What is AI Emaily?

AI Emaily is an AI-native email client with an autonomous assistant that triages your inbox, drafts replies in your voice, schedules meetings and closes loops. It unifies Gmail, Outlook and other providers in one inbox on every device.

It is built for busy professionals and teams who want an agent to handle email work while they stay in control, with undo and an audit trail on actions.

Listed in our personal agents category. Official site: aiemaily.com.

Key features

  • Works with every major email provider in one unified inbox
  • Inbox triage and voice-matched reply drafting
  • Scheduling and follow-ups handled by the assistant
  • Autopilot mode that can act on email on its own
  • Filters cold pitches and noise into a digest
  • BYOK, multi-model and MCP tools on Autopilot and Team plans

AI Emaily pricing

Free plan available. Paid plans from $14.99/month. 7-day free trial on paid plans.
