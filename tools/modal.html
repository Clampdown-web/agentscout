About Modal

Modal is listed in our MCP & infra category and is aimed at builders. Serverless compute and sandboxes for AI workloads. Pricing, as the maker states it: Usage-based, free credits. Maturity: Mature. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-14.

What is Modal?

Modal is cloud infrastructure for teams that develop, train and serve AI applications at scale.

Its products include inference for serving LLMs, image and video models; our listing also notes serverless compute and sandboxes.

Sources: the official site and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: modal.com.
