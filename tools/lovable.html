About Lovable

Lovable is the AI app builder at lovable.dev. It is listed next to Lovable MCP, the official MCP server for working with Lovable projects from another agent. Lovable workspaces can import a skill ZIP under Workspace settings, Skills, Add, Upload ZIP, and they can connect a registry that serves GET /v0/servers.

Pricing, as the maker states it: free tier and paid plans on the official site. This page is the product. The MCP server listing stays on its own page.
