What is PipesHub?

PipesHub is an open-source platform for connecting AI applications to knowledge stored across your company's systems.

It connects Slack, Google Drive, GitHub, Microsoft 365, Notion and 50+ other systems, so you can search across them and get cited answers.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: pipeshub.com.

Companies that want agents and search over internal knowledge.
