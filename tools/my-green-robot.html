What is My Green Robot?

My Green Robot is a consumer AI phone and email agent built for Rhode Island homeowners who want local lawn care, landscaping, tree service, irrigation, hardscaping or snow removal quotes without playing phone tag. You describe the yard job (or upload a yard photo for AI scope analysis), set a target budget and add any custom questions, and the robot finds three well-rated local contractors on Google.

During business hours, the AI agent phones and emails those three crews on your behalf to explain the job, ask your questions, and arrange free on-site estimates or collect pricing. Every call recording and live transcript is saved in your dashboard so you can compare quotes side by side, and strict rate limits ensure no local business is called more than once a week.

Sources: mygreenrobot.com, mygreenrobot.com/pricing and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-24.
