About Cleric

Cleric is listed in our coding category and is aimed at builders. AI SRE agent that investigates production alerts and finds root causes. Pricing, as the maker states it: Enterprise. Maturity: Active. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-16.

What is Cleric?

Cleric is an AI SRE agent that investigates production alerts and finds root causes. Its agents follow every change after merge, verify its effect under real production traffic, investigate failures, hand off fixes and reverify recovery.

The company says Cleric was named a Gartner Cool Vendor in AI for SRE and Observability. Pricing is enterprise.

Sources: the official site and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: cleric.ai.

Who is Cleric for?

SRE and platform teams at companies that want to catch regressions from deployments before customers notice.
