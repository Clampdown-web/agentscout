About Open Connector

Open Connector is listed in our MCP & infra category and is aimed at builders. Open-source auth gateway connecting SaaS apps to AI agents via SDK, CLI and MCP. Pricing, as the maker states it: Open source. Maturity: Mature. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-16.

What is Open Connector?

Open Connector is an open-source connector gateway for AI agents and an alternative to Pipedream or Composio. Users connect app accounts once, and agents get a shared catalog of 1,000+ providers.

There is a managed option with hosted OAuth and runtime, or you can run it yourself with Docker or Node.js and manage storage and OAuth. Our listing notes access via SDK, CLI and MCP.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: oomol.com.

Who is Open Connector for?

Developers who need agents to act in users' SaaS accounts without building auth for each app.
