About Invoke

Invoke is listed in our image generation category and is aimed at people who want a ready-to-use product. Open-source layered canvas for Stable Diffusion and FLUX, with inpainting, control layers and node workflows. Pricing, as the maker states it: Free and open source (Apache-2.0), self-hosted; the hosted service closed in Oct 2025. Maturity: Active. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-19.

What is Invoke?

Invoke is an open-source creative engine for generating and refining images with AI, with a web-based UI that also underpins several commercial products. It is free to use under a commercially friendly licence and installs on your own hardware through the Invoke launcher.

Its Unified Canvas brings generation, inpainting and outpainting, brush tools and control layers into one space, and node-based Workflows let you build and share custom pipelines. Boards and a gallery keep images and their prompts organized, and a Model Manager handles checkpoints, diffusers and some GGUF models.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-19. Official site: invoke.ai.

Who is Invoke for?

Artists and studios who want a polished local app for Stable Diffusion and FLUX image work, with both a canvas and node workflows.
