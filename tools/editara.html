What is Editara?

Editara is an online AI photo editor built around plain-language instructions rather than manual layers or brush masks. Users drop a JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC or TIFF image into the browser—or batch-upload multiple photos with one instruction—and describe the change they want, such as removing a person and their shadow, replacing or clearing a background, restoring creases and faded colors in old prints, colorizing black-and-white photos, extending an image to a new aspect ratio or upscaling up to 4K.

The editor also includes text-to-image generation and multi-photo combination, and lets users choose between eight underlying models per run: GPT Image 2 (4+ credits), Seedream 4 (8+ credits), Seedream 5 Lite (10+ credits), Nano Banana (11+ credits), Seedream 4.5 (11+ credits), Nano Banana 2 (16+ credits), Seedream 5 Pro (18+ credits) and Nano Banana Pro (40+ credits). New visitors get 12 free credits immediately without creating an account, signed-in free accounts receive 12 fresh credits every day, exports never carry a watermark, and uploaded originals are deleted after processing.
