What is Momen?

Momen is a no-code full-stack app builder. You visually build the frontend, database, backend logic (Actionflows), APIs and AI agents, then publish to your own domain.

It is for founders and makers who want a production app with real data and payments without writing code, and it supports bringing your own AI model on paid plans.

Listed in our app builders category. Official site: momen.app.

Key features

  • Visual builder for frontend, database and backend logic
  • Built-in AI agents with bring-your-own-model on paid plans
  • Automated Actionflows on schedules and database triggers
  • APIs, payments, SSO and custom domains
  • Usage-based add-ons for storage, data transfer and AI points
  • Dedicated instance and BYOC deployment options
