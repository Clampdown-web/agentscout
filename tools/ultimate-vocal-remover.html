What is Ultimate Vocal Remover?

Ultimate Vocal Remover (UVR) is a free, open-source desktop app that uses state-of-the-art source separation models to remove vocals from audio files or split them into stems. Its core developers, Anjok07 and aufr33, trained all the bundled models except the Demucs v3 and v4 4-stem models.

The installers bundle the interface, Python, PyTorch and other dependencies, so no prerequisites are needed. There are Windows installers (including a DirectML build for AMD Radeon and Intel Arc GPUs), a macOS build and manual install options for Linux.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-19. Official site: github.com/Anjok07/ultimatevocalremovergui.

Who is Ultimate Vocal Remover for?

Musicians, DJs, karaoke makers and video editors who want high-quality vocal and instrument separation on their own computer for free.
