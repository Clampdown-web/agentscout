Screenpipe is listed in our personal agents category and is aimed at builders. Records your screen and audio locally so AI agents can search and act on your computer history. What is Screenpipe?

Screenpipe continuously records computer work (screen and audio) locally to map workflows, find work worth automating and give agents context.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: screenpipe.com.
