About YRIKKA Text-to-Model

YRIKKA Text-to-Model is listed in our vision category and is aimed at builders. YRIKKA's ORION workflow that turns a plain-language mission description and a few unlabeled images into generated training data, candidate models and an evaluated, selected vision model. Pricing, as the maker states it: Enterprise. Maturity: Active. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-17.

What is YRIKKA Text-to-Model?

YRIKKA's ORION workflow automates data generation and training: it personalizes a generator with parameter-efficient fine-tuning on about 10-50 examples and auto-annotates the generated data.

The output is labeled, multimodal (e.g. EO/IR) data matched to your optics and environment; our listing notes it starts from a plain-language mission description.

Sources: the official site and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: yrikka.com/text-to-model.
