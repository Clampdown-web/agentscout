    What is Google Deep Review?

    Google Deep Review is a Google Research multi-agent system for analyzing technical documents.

    Google says it has been deployed across 16,000+ submissions at conferences including STOC, ICML, NeurIPS and ICLR; our listing notes it checks papers, design docs and legal or financial documents for logical gaps and unsupported claims.

    Sources: the official site and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: research.google/blog/analyzing-technical-documents-with-deep-review.
