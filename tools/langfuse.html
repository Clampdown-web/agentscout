About Langfuse

Langfuse is listed in our evals & observability category and is aimed at builders. Open-source LLM observability, tracing and evals. Pricing, as the maker states it: Open source + paid cloud. Maturity: Mature. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-14.

What is Langfuse?

Langfuse is an open-source LLM engineering platform that helps teams develop, monitor, evaluate and debug AI applications together.

It can be self-hosted in minutes, with a paid cloud as well.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: langfuse.com.

Who is Langfuse for?

Teams building LLM apps who want open-source tracing and evals.

Langfuse pricing

  • Hobby: Free
  • Core: $29/mo · 100k units a month, 90 days data access
  • Pro: $199/mo · unlimited history
  • Enterprise: $2499/mo

Additional usage is $8 per 100k units. A Teams add-on is $300/mo. Langfuse can also be self-hosted from its GitHub repo.

Source: official pricing page, checked Oct 18, 2026. Prices change; check the source before buying.
