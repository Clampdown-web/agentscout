What is AI Music Mastering?

AI Music Mastering is a browser-based automated mastering studio designed for independent musicians, home-studio engineers and creators finishing DAW recordings or AI-generated songs. You upload a completed mono or stereo mix in WAV, MP3, FLAC or AIFF format (up to 200 MB and 10 minutes long) and shape its tonal balance, punch, stereo width and loudness right in the browser.

The studio provides eight distinct mastering sound profiles—Universal, Fire, Clarity, Tape, Natural, Spatial, Cinematic and Punch—paired with adjustable intensity and EQ controls plus loudness-matched A/B playback so you can compare tonal and dynamic changes fairly against your original mix. Previewing profiles and tuning EQ is free; an active monthly membership unlocks reference-track matching, full song history and mastered exports in 24-bit WAV, 16-bit WAV and 320 kbps MP3.
