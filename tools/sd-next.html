    What is SD.Next?

    SD.Next, by Vladimir Mandic, is an all-in-one open-source web interface for generating and editing images and video with a wide range of diffusion models. It runs locally on NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, Apple Silicon or CPU setups, on desktop or mobile.

    You can create images or video from text or images, inpaint, outpaint and refine, guide generation with LoRA, ControlNet and IP-Adapters, and caption, tag, upscale, interpolate or colorize. Models can be auto-downloaded from a reference list, SDNQ quantization and offloading help on smaller GPUs, and a built-in installer manages dependencies and updates.

    Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-21. Official site: vladmandic.github.io/sdnext.
