BrowserOS is listed in our browser/computer use category and is aimed at people who want a ready-to-use product. Open-source agentic browser that runs AI agents locally. Pricing, as the maker states it: Open source. Maturity: Mature. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-16.

What is BrowserOS?

BrowserOS is a free, open-source second browser just for your AI agents, with everything running on your machine.

You can import Chrome logins in one click, connect Claude Code, Codex or any MCP agent, and let agents run in parallel tabs while you watch live or replay.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: browseros.com.
