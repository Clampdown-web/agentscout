    About MyShell

    MyShell is listed in our no-code builders category and is aimed at people who want a ready-to-use product. Platform to build, share and use AI agents and apps. Pricing, as the maker states it: Freemium. Maturity: Active. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-16.

    What is MyShell?

    MyShell describes itself as an AI consumer layer where people can build, share and own AI agents. It has its own $SHELL token. The site is currently built around MyShell Art, launched on June 4, 2026, which creates portraits, filters and short videos from a selfie.

    MyShell Art is powered by Shell Agent and offers 200+ styles across professional, artistic, fashion and trending categories. Examples include headshot generators, storyboard and one-image film generators, cartoon and pixel-art styles, and virtual try-ons. MyShell says you can start creating for free.

    Sources: myshell.ai and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18.

    Who is MyShell for?

    Casual creators who want quick AI portraits, filters and short videos, and builders interested in MyShell's agent and token ecosystem.
