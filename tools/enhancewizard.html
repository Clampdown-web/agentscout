What is EnhanceWizard?

EnhanceWizard is a browser-based photo enhancer and upscaler built around two processing paths: a free local browser mode and optional paid cloud models. In the free tier, images up to 16 MP (8,192 pixels per side) run directly inside your browser on your own device, so photos are not uploaded to a server, require no account or credit card, and export without watermarks or daily count limits.

Beyond general 2x and 4x upscaling, EnhanceWizard includes task-specific workflows for unblurring, noise reduction, old photo restoration, black-and-white photo colorization, portrait enhancement, real estate and e-commerce product shots, anime and pixel art, plus print utilities such as a 300 DPI converter and image splitter. When a photo needs heavier reconstruction or batch processing up to 10 images at once, users can spend cloud credits on Standard HD (1–4 credits per image) or AI Retouch (4–7 credits per image).

Sources: enhancewizard.com, enhancewizard.com/pricing and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-24.
