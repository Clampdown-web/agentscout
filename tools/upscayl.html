About Upscayl

Upscayl is listed in our photography & photo editing category and is aimed at people who want a ready-to-use product. Free open-source desktop app for upscaling images on your own computer. Pricing, as the maker states it: Free and open source (AGPL-3.0) desktop app; Upscayl Cloud free trial (10 credits), Pro $24.99/mo. Maturity: Active. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-19.

What is Upscayl?

Upscayl is a free and open-source desktop app that enlarges and enhances low-resolution images with AI upscaling models, running on your own computer.

It is available for Linux (AppImage, Flatpak, Snap, DEB, RPM), macOS 12+ (including Homebrew) and Windows 10+, and needs a Vulkan-compatible GPU. A separate paid service, Upscayl Cloud, upscales in the browser with credits.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-19. Official site: upscayl.org.

Who is Upscayl for?

Photographers, designers and anyone who wants to upscale images locally for free, or in the cloud without a capable GPU.
