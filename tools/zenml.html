About ZenML

ZenML is listed in our frameworks category and is aimed at builders. MLOps and agent platform for building pipelines and deploying agents. Pricing, as the maker states it: Open source + cloud. Maturity: Mature. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-16.

What is ZenML?

ZenML is built for ML and AI engineers working on traditional ML, LLM workflows or agents in a company setting. At its core you write workflows (pipelines) that can be run and tracked.

Our listing describes it as an MLOps and agent platform for building pipelines and deploying agents, with a cloud option.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: zenml.io.

Who is ZenML for?

ML and AI engineering teams that want reproducible pipelines for models and agents.
