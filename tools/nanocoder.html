What is Nanocoder?

Nanocoder is an open coding agent for your terminal, built by the Nano Collective, a community collective rather than a company. You bring your own model and keep your code on your machine.

It runs on local models via Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible API, such as OpenRouter, Requesty, Anthropic and Google. The project says there are no closed-source features and no paid tiers gating useful parts.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: docs.nanocollective.org/nanocoder.
