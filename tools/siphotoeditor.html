What is SiPhotoEditor?

SiPhotoEditor is a browser-based AI photo editor where you upload an image and type the edit you want in plain English—such as replacing a cluttered background, swapping an outfit for a professional headshot, changing daytime lighting to golden hour, or removing people in the background—without manual masking or layer editing.

Before running an edit, users can pick from eight underlying image-editing models ranging from fast 9-credit runs (Seedream 5 Flash, Grok Imagine Edit, Qwen Image Edit) to higher-detail 19-to-45-credit runs (Nano Banana 2, Flux 2 Dev/Pro, Seedream 4.5 and Nano Banana Pro). Every result opens in a side-by-side before-and-after comparator and supports iterative follow-up instructions on the edited image.

Sources: siphotoeditor.com, siphotoeditor.com/pricing and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-24.
