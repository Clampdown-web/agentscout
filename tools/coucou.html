What is coucou?

Coucou is a small open-source companion that lives in your Mac's notch, or at the top of the screen on Windows and Linux, and keeps an eye on your AI coding agent sessions. Its mascot Mochi pops out when Claude Code needs you, and you can see what each agent reads, edits and runs, approve permissions, drop in a file or chat with Claude without switching windows.

It follows Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Gemini CLI, Antigravity, Copilot CLI, OpenCode, Amp and other agents, and when you step away it moves to your iPhone or Android phone so you can approve from the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island. Version 0.4 also made it a full Mac app with hold-to-talk dictation (offline with NVIDIA Parakeet) and a call notetaker that needs no meeting bot.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-22. Official site: louis-cfm.github.io/coucou.

    Compozy

    Operating system for AI agents that turns Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and Cursor into a team with task handoff, loops, jobs and shared memory.

    Wake

    Wake is a desktop app that collects every coding-agent conversation on your computer into one searchable place. It reads the local history kept by Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Copilot CLI, Gemini CLI, Antigravity, Devin and other agents, groups sessions by agent and project, and lets you find a past conversation in seconds and resume it in your terminal. It is read-only and nothing leaves your machine. Built in Rust, it runs on macOS, with Linux and Windows in beta.
