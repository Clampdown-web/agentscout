    Cuebric alternatives

    Other film & TV production tools in our directory. See all film & TV production tools →

    Lightcraft Jetset

    iPhone app for virtual production. Track the camera and shoot actors against live 3D sets or Gaussian splat locations, then send the data to post.

    Intangible

    AI 3D studio for previs and concept work. Block out scenes in 3D, set cameras and turn them into images and video.

    Autodesk Flow Studio

    Autodesk's cloud AI VFX tool, formerly Wonder Studio. Turn live-action footage into editable CG scenes with markerless mocap, camera tracking, clean plates and masks for Maya, Blender or Unreal.

    Concat

    Concat is a free, open-source video editor meant as a CapCut replacement that runs entirely on your own machine. It covers auto-captions, text-to-speech voices, background removal, keyframes, titles, effects, filters and transitions with multi-track editing up to 4K, with no watermark, account or subscription. It runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS, and it can be scripted through a documented JSON-RPC and gRPC API, so scripts and AI agents can drive edits.

    Adobe Premiere AI Assistant

    Chat panel inside Adobe Premiere (public beta). Ask it in plain words to sort footage into bins, transcribe, find slates, add markers and build stringouts or rough assemblies. Every step can be undone.

    Axle AI

    AI media asset management for post houses and broadcasters. Tags faces, objects and speech so editors can search footage.
