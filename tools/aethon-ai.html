    Wavegrain

    Wavegrain makes curated TikTok slideshows and Instagram carousels and can schedule them from an assistant.     Lanesra AI

    Lanesra AI is an analytics layer for AI search that tracks how ChatGPT, Gemini and other answer engines describe, rank and recommend your brand against named competitors, then suggests fixes for AEO, GEO and agent experience; plans from about €150 a month with a 3-day free trial.

    Venbit Content Agent

    Venbit Content Agent is an AI writer for small-business websites: it reads your site so the article sounds like your business, plans the piece with you, offers two outlines, then writes the one you pick, hero image included, ready to copy, download or publish. Your first article is free. Venbit also runs a free AI Visibility check that asks ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Gemini who they recommend for your trade and city and scores each answer. It is part of Venbit's software for local businesses, whose plans start at $39 a month after a one-month trial.

    Adgent

    Adgent is an AI marketing coworker for a team. The homepage describes campaign work, planning, dashboards and a read of what AI answers say about a brand. On 25 Nov 2026 the pricing page had no public price. It asked visitors to talk to sales and request a demo.

    InsightFuse

    InsightFuse is an AI PR agent for brands and agencies. You say what you are announcing; it finds journalists who cover similar stories from a database of 799,000+ profiles, writes each one a personalized pitch, sends it from your own Gmail or Outlook and tracks every reply in one place. Searching journalists is free; the PR agent starts at $49 a month (Starter) and Pro at $149 adds unlimited campaigns and background autopilot, with no per-pitch charges.

    GMapsScraper.AI

    GMapsScraper.AI is an AI-powered Google Maps scraper for lead generation: enter keywords and it pulls business listings with names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, social links, ratings, reviews, photos and opening hours, exported to CSV or JSON. It also offers a REST API and a remote MCP server (/api/mcp) so AI agents in Claude, Cursor or OpenAI can run Google Maps searches directly.
