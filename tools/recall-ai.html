About Recall.ai

Recall.ai is listed in our MCP & infra category and is aimed at builders. API for meeting bots that join Zoom, Meet and Teams to record and transcribe. Pricing, as the maker states it: Usage-based. Maturity: Active. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-16.

What is Recall.ai?

Recall.ai is an API for getting transcripts, recordings and metadata from meetings, and the infrastructure behind many meeting-recording products.

It captures each person's audio separately so transcripts show who said what, even when people talk over each other.

Sources: the official site and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: recall.ai.

Who is Recall.ai for?

Developers building meeting bots or note-takers for Zoom, Meet and Teams.

  • Meeting bots for Zoom, Meet and Teams
  • Recordings and transcripts
  • Per-speaker audio
