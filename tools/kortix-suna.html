    About Kortix Suna

    Kortix Suna is listed in our personal agents category and is aimed at builders. Kortix, an open-source platform for building and running general-purpose AI agents. Pricing, as the maker states it: Source-available + cloud. Maturity: Mature. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-16.

    What is Kortix Suna?

    Kortix (Suna) is a source-available platform for agents that deliver finished work such as reports, decks, code, replies and deployed changes.

    It pitches itself as an open-source alternative to Claude Cowork and ChatGPT Work.

    Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: github.com/kortix-ai/suna.
