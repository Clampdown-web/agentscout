    What is 9bot?

    9bot is a WhatsApp automation platform from Brazil for people who run groups, communities and Channels: creators, news portals, educators and companies. It sends recurring messages and news from RSS feeds and APIs, answers custom commands, welcomes new members and posts to WhatsApp Channels, with live-stream alerts for Twitch, YouTube and Kick and scheduled affiliate posts for Mercado Livre, Amazon and Shopee.

    Moderation rules handle spam, suspicious links, banned words and a configurable punishment ladder, and a dashboard reports engagement, peak activity and growth. Its official MCP server (remote, OAuth sign-in with your WhatsApp number) lets compatible AI assistants and agents manage groups, schedule messages, moderate and read metrics for your 9bot account. An active 9bot subscription is required.
