Yao is listed in our personal agents category and is aimed at builders. Workspace for running and tracking all your agents and tasks across devices. Pricing, as the maker states it: Source-available. Maturity: Mature. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-16.

What is Yao?

Yao puts all your agents and workspaces in one place, on every device you own, with tasks tracked on a board.

It is accessible from desktop, mobile, browser or API, and self-hosted.
