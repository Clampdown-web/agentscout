    About Devin

    Devin is listed in our coding category and is aimed at builders. Cognition's autonomous software engineer that takes tickets and opens PRs. Pricing, as the maker states it: Free plan; Pro $20/mo; Max $200/mo; Teams $80/mo + $40/seat. Maturity: Mature. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-18.

    What is Devin?

    Devin is Cognition's autonomous AI software engineer. It runs in the cloud or on your machine, tests its work in its own browser and keeps going until a pull request is ready to merge. You can start it from Devin Cloud, from Devin Desktop or the CLI, or by tagging @Devin in Slack or Teams.

    Beyond writing code, it can run automations that react to bugs, failed CI and alerts. Devin Wiki maps a codebase and its architecture, and Devin Review checks pull requests for bugs and vulnerabilities. On the official pricing page, the Free plan comes with a light quota. Pro costs $20 a month and adds Devin Cloud, Max costs $200 a month, and Teams costs $80 a month plus $40 for each full developer seat. Enterprise plans add SAML/OIDC SSO and a VPC deployment option.

    Sources: devin.ai, devin.ai/pricing and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18.

    Who is Devin for?

    Engineering teams that want to hand off tickets, bug fixes, migrations and reviews to an agent that opens pull requests, while keeping humans in the review loop.

    Key features

    • Runs in the cloud (Devin Cloud) or locally (Devin Desktop and CLI)
    • Tests changes in its own browser before opening a PR
    • Tag @Devin in Slack and Teams; Linear and Jira integrations
    • Automations for bugs, failed CI and alerts
    • Devin Wiki codebase maps and Devin Review PR checks
    • Free plan; Pro $20/mo, Max $200/mo, Teams $80/mo plus $40 per seat

    Devin pricing

    • Free: $0/mo · light agent quota, unlimited inline edits and Tab completions
    • Pro: $20/mo · higher quotas, frontier models from OpenAI, Claude, Gemini and SpaceXAI
    • Max: $200/mo · much higher quotas than Pro
    • Teams: $80/mo · plus $40/mo per full dev seat, up to 200 users
    • Enterprise: Custom

    Extra usage on paid plans is billed at API pricing.
