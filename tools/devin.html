What is Devin?

Devin is Cognition's autonomous AI software engineer. It runs in the cloud or on your machine, tests its work in its own browser and keeps going until a pull request is ready to merge. You can start it from Devin Cloud, from Devin Desktop or the CLI, or by tagging @Devin in Slack or Teams.

Beyond writing code, it can run automations that react to bugs, failed CI and alerts. Devin Wiki maps a codebase and its architecture, and Devin Review checks pull requests for bugs and vulnerabilities. On the official pricing page, the Free plan comes with a light quota. Pro costs $20 a month and adds Devin Cloud, Max costs $200 a month, and Teams costs $80 a month plus $40 for each full developer seat. Enterprise plans add SAML/OIDC SSO and a VPC deployment option.

Sources: devin.ai, devin.ai/pricing and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18.
