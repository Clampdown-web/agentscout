    What is FunASR?

    FunASR is an industrial speech-recognition toolkit from ModelScope for offline, streaming and edge deployment.

    It covers speech recognition, voice activity detection, punctuation, speaker pipelines and emotion and audio-event models, and can serve models through an OpenAI-compatible API. Fun-ASR-Nano can also run through Hugging Face Transformers.

    Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: github.com/modelscope/FunASR.

    Who is FunASR for?

    Developers building voice agents or transcription features who want self-hosted speech recognition, including streaming and edge setups.
