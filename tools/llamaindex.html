About LlamaIndex

LlamaIndex is listed in our frameworks category and is aimed at builders. Framework for agents over your data (RAG, document agents). Pricing, as the maker states it: Open source + paid cloud. Maturity: Mature. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-14.

What is LlamaIndex?

LlamaIndex is a framework for building agents and RAG applications over your data. Its maintainers say their current focus is the best AI-powered engine for document parsing and extraction.

LlamaParse is the company's enterprise platform for agentic OCR, parsing, extraction and indexing, and LiteParse is a free, fast text parser. The open-source framework has offered orchestration tools for RAG and agent apps since 2023.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: llamaindex.ai.

Who is LlamaIndex for?

Developers building document agents and RAG over their own files, especially where document parsing quality matters.
