What is Openfort?

Openfort is open-source wallet infrastructure for stablecoin products: dollar accounts, spending policies, funding and agent wallets.

It positions itself as a self-hostable Privy alternative; our listing notes gas sponsorship and x402 support.

Sources: the official site and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: openfort.io.
