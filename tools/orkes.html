About Orkes

Orkes is listed in our frameworks category and is aimed at builders. Orchestration platform built on Conductor for durable workflows and AI agents. Pricing, as the maker states it: Open source + cloud. Maturity: Mature. We last checked the official link on 2026-10-16.

What is Orkes?

Orkes builds on Conductor, an open-source durable execution platform for microservices, AI agents and adaptive workflow graphs. Conductor originated at Netflix and is maintained by Orkes and the community.

It turns runtime choices such as loops, branching, fan-out, tool calls, approvals, retries and cancellation into durable, inspectable execution. Running it locally needs Node.js 16+ and Java 21+, and Orkes offers a cloud platform.

Sources: the official README and our directory listing, checked 2026-10-18. Official site: orkes.io.

Who is Orkes for?

Engineering teams that need reliable, long-running workflows and agents with retries, approvals and visibility.
