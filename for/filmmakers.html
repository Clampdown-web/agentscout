Storyboarding and previs

Tools that turn a script into storyboards, shot lists, animatics and 3D previs, plus script breakdowns and coverage for pre-production. All 10 tools →

  • DramaClaw See site
    Self-hosted AI video pipeline that goes from script to storyboard, assets and finished film for short dramas, ads and product videos. · ★ 6,490
  • CozyClay Open source
    Free browser-based 3D previs software: block a scene, pose characters and author camera moves and cuts, then hand the same shots to an AI video model. · ★ 1,117
  • Frametell Open source + cloud
    Frametell turns an idea, PDF, slides or notes into a narrated, hand-drawn explainer film: a director agent storyboards it with you for approval, writes and voices the script and draws every page, as an open-source Mac app or Frametell Cloud. · ★ 418
  • Boords Paid
    Storyboard and animatic tool with an AI storyboard generator. Plan scripts and shots, share them with clients and collect feedback and sign-off in one place.
  • Filmustage Freemium
    AI pre-production platform. Upload a script to get scene breakdowns, schedules, budgets and call sheets in any language or format.
  • Greenlight Coverage Paid
    Upload a screenplay and get script coverage in minutes, with notes on plot, character, dialogue and marketability.

Rough cuts

Assistant editors that log footage, pick selects, remove bad takes and silences and assemble a first cut you can finish in your editor. All 10 tools →

  • Adobe Premiere AI Assistant Paid
    Chat panel inside Adobe Premiere (public beta). Ask it in plain words to sort footage into bins, transcribe, find slates, add markers and build stringouts or rough assemblies. Every step can be undone.
  • AutoPod Paid
    Premiere Pro plugin for automatic multicam editing, jump cuts and social exports of podcasts and talk shows.
  • Cleanvoice Freemium
    AI that removes filler words, mouth sounds, silences and background noise from speech recordings.
  • Cutback Paid
    Premiere Pro assistant that auto-edits raw footage and multicam: picks selects, cuts silences, transcribes and translates.
  • Descript Freemium
    Video and audio editor you edit like a doc. Its Underlord co-editor can cut filler words, build rough cuts, add captions, clean up audio and write show notes when you ask it to.
  • Eddie AI Usage-based
    AI assistant editor for interviews and documentary footage. Chat with it, or connect it to ChatGPT or Claude over MCP, to get rough cuts you can export to Premiere, Resolve or Final Cut.

VFX and compositing

AI for visual effects: rotoscoping and masks, relighting, compositing, tracking, markerless mocap and virtual production. All 14 tools →

  • AIVA Freemium
    AI composition assistant for soundtrack and score music in many styles, with MIDI export and editing.
  • Autodesk Flow Studio Freemium
    Autodesk's cloud AI VFX tool, formerly Wonder Studio. Turn live-action footage into editable CG scenes with markerless mocap, camera tracking, clean plates and masks for Maya, Blender or Unreal.
  • Beeble Paid
    AI VFX for filmmakers. Relight footage after the shoot by pulling normal, depth and other passes from ordinary video.
  • Boris FX Mocha Pro Paid
    Academy Award-winning planar tracking, roto and object removal plugin, with ML-based masking, for After Effects, Premiere, Resolve, Nuke and more.
  • Cuebric See site
    Generative AI tool for virtual production. Make layered 2.5D backgrounds for LED volumes and greenscreen from concept to camera in minutes.
  • DaVinci Resolve Freemium
    Blackmagic's editing, color, VFX and audio post app. Its AI tools include IntelliSearch, Magic Mask, voice isolation, speed warp and dialogue leveling.

Video post-production

Picture post in one place: editing and assistant-editor agents, subtitles, color, VFX, upscaling and visual dubbing, grouped by stage. All 69 tools →

  • OpenMontage Open source
    Open-source agentic video production system with ready pipelines and tools that agents use to edit and assemble videos. · ★ 72,332
  • Hypit Freemium
    An open-source agent workflow that takes a viral short video and rebuilds it as an editable template, letting you swap the presenter, script, voice and B-roll to produce many new variants with your own API keys. · ★ 26,010
  • Palmier Pro Freemium
    macOS video editor built around AI, where an assistant can search footage, assemble cuts and edit the timeline for you. · ★ 14,639
  • YuE Open source
    Open song-generation model (YuE2) with lyrics-to-song, zero-shot covers and agentic music editing. · ★ 12,780
  • Concat Open source
    Concat is a free, open-source video editor meant as a CapCut replacement that runs entirely on your own machine. It covers auto-captions, text-to-speech voices, background removal, keyframes, titles, effects, filters and transitions with multi-track editing up to 4K, with no watermark, account or subscription. It runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS, and it can be scripted through a documented JSON-RPC and gRPC API, so scripts and AI agents can drive edits. · ★ 11,893
  • ACE-Step Open source
    Open music generation foundation model for fast full-song generation and editing. · ★ 4,993

Audio post-production

Sound for picture, grouped by stage: dialogue cleanup, ADR and dubbing voices, Foley and sound effects, and the editors and mixing tools that tie it together. All 34 tools →

  • AudioCraft Open source
    Meta's open library for audio generation, including MusicGen for music and AudioGen for sound effects. · ★ 23,762
  • MMAudio Open source
    Open model that generates synced sound effects and Foley from video, from the CVPR 2025 paper. · ★ 2,299
  • HunyuanVideo-Foley Open weights
    Tencent's open model that generates high-fidelity Foley audio matched to video. · ★ 1,083
  • Accentize See site
    AI dialogue restoration plugins for post-production: speech enhancement, de-reverb, noise reduction and reverb matching for ADR.
  • Acon Digital Extract:Dialogue Paid
    AI dialogue cleaning plugin with real-time reverb reduction and simple mixer controls.
  • AI Music Mastering Freemium
    AI Music Mastering is a browser-based automated audio mastering studio for finished recordings, DAW mixes and AI-generated songs. Upload WAV, MP3, FLAC or AIFF tracks up to 200 MB and 10 minutes long, audition eight sound profiles (Universal, Fire, Clarity, Tape, Natural, Spatial, Cinematic and Punch) with adjustable intensity, EQ and loudness-matched A/B previews for free, and export 24-bit WAV, 16-bit WAV or 320 kbps MP3 masters with a membership.

Dubbing and lip sync

  • BaoCut See site
    An AI video agent for macOS and Windows that transcribes, subtitles, translates, dubs, cuts and animates your footage from plain instructions, with every result landing in an editable timeline instead of a one-shot export. · ★ 1,545
  • AudioShake See site
    AI audio separation. Split dialogue, music and effects (DME) or music stems for dubbing, remixes and clean-up.
  • CAMB.AI Freemium
    AI dubbing, live translation and lifelike voices in 150+ languages for films, broadcasts and sport.
  • Captions Paid
    AI video editor and generator for creators, with auto captions, eye contact, dubbing and AI edits.
  • Cutback Paid
    Premiere Pro assistant that auto-edits raw footage and multicam: picks selects, cuts silences, transcribes and translates.
  • Deepdub Enterprise
    AI dubbing for studios and streamers, with emotional voice cloning and localization in many languages.
