  • Acon Digital Extract:Dialogue Paid
    AI dialogue cleaning plugin with real-time reverb reduction and simple mixer controls.
  • AI Music Mastering Freemium
    AI Music Mastering is a browser-based automated audio mastering studio for finished recordings, DAW mixes and AI-generated songs. Upload WAV, MP3, FLAC or AIFF tracks up to 200 MB and 10 minutes long, audition eight sound profiles (Universal, Fire, Clarity, Tape, Natural, Spatial, Cinematic and Punch) with adjustable intensity, EQ and loudness-matched A/B previews for free, and export 24-bit WAV, 16-bit WAV or 320 kbps MP3 masters with a membership.
  • AutoTune Paid
    Antares' pitch-correction plugins plus AI-powered vocal mixing tools for vocal tuning and effects.
  • BandLab Freemium
    Free cloud music-making platform and DAW with AI tools such as SongStarter and AI mastering, plus collaboration.
  • CryoMix Freemium
    Online AI mixing and mastering for vocals, beats and stems, with a free full-length preview.
  • eMastered Freemium
    Online AI mastering engine created by Grammy-winning engineers.

Stem separation

Split a mix into vocals, drums, bass and other stems, or dialogue, music and effects (DME), for remixing, karaoke, dubbing and clean-up. All 21 tools →

  • Spleeter Open source
    Deezer's source separation library with pretrained models for splitting songs into stems. · ★ 28,498
  • Ultimate Vocal Remover Open source
    Free desktop app that separates vocals and instruments with deep neural networks. · ★ 26,859
  • Antinode Clear Paid
    AI plugin that separates voice, reverb and noise in real time so you can rebalance dialogue (formerly Supertone Clear).
  • Audio AI Studio Freemium
    Audio AI Studio is a browser voice studio with 400+ voices in 36 languages, voice cloning, AI music, stem splitting and transcription.
  • AudioShake See site
    AI audio separation. Split dialogue, music and effects (DME) or music stems for dubbing, remixes and clean-up.
  • CryoMix Freemium
    Online AI mixing and mastering for vocals, beats and stems, with a free full-length preview.
