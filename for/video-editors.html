Rough cuts

Assistant editors that log footage, pick selects, remove bad takes and silences and assemble a first cut you can finish in your editor. All 12 tools →

  • ChatCut Freemium
    ChatCut is an AI video editor for agents: its MCP server and plugins let Codex, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Cursor and others edit your footage, add captions, remove silences and filler words, create motion graphics, generate voiceovers and music and export an editable timeline. · ★ 1,017
  • Adobe Premiere AI Assistant Paid
    Chat panel inside Adobe Premiere (public beta). Ask it in plain words to sort footage into bins, transcribe, find slates, add markers and build stringouts or rough assemblies. Every step can be undone.
  • AutoPod Paid
    Premiere Pro plugin for automatic multicam editing, jump cuts and social exports of podcasts and talk shows.
  • Cleanvoice Freemium
    AI that removes filler words, mouth sounds, silences and background noise from speech recordings.
  • Cutback Paid
    Premiere Pro assistant that auto-edits raw footage and multicam: picks selects, cuts silences, transcribes and translates.
  • Descript Freemium
    Video and audio editor you edit like a doc. Its Underlord co-editor can cut filler words, build rough cuts, add captions, clean up audio and write show notes when you ask it to.

Video post-production

Picture post in one place: editing and assistant-editor agents, subtitles, color, VFX, upscaling and visual dubbing, grouped by stage. All 88 tools →

  • OpenMontage Open source
    OpenMontage is an AGPL-3.0 agentic video production system that turns an AI coding assistant such as Claude Code, Cursor or Codex into a video studio: you describe the video in plain language and the agent runs research, scripting, asset generation, editing and final composition through ready-made pipelines. Besides image-based videos it can cut real motion edits from free stock footage and open archives, and the repo ships a review guide and an agent guide. Paid image and video generation providers are optional. · ★ 73,179
  • Hypit Open source
    An open-source agent workflow that takes a viral short video and rebuilds it as an editable template, letting you swap the presenter, script, voice and B-roll to produce many new variants with your own API keys. · ★ 26,753
  • Palmier Pro Freemium
    macOS video editor built around AI, where an assistant can search footage, assemble cuts and edit the timeline for you. · ★ 14,659
  • OpenCreator Open source
    OpenCreator, formerly KrillinAI, is an Apache-2.0 workspace for creators that runs locally and brings reusable skills and agents together for scripts, video, images, voice, translation and editing, building on its original strength of translating and dubbing videos into more than 120 languages, with a desktop app. · ★ 14,288
  • YuE Open source
    Open song-generation model (YuE2) with lyrics-to-song, zero-shot covers and agentic music editing. · ★ 12,904
  • Concat Open source
    Concat is a free, open-source video editor meant as a CapCut replacement that runs entirely on your own machine. It covers auto-captions, text-to-speech voices, background removal, keyframes, titles, effects, filters and transitions with multi-track editing up to 4K, with no watermark, account or subscription. It runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS, and it can be scripted through a documented JSON-RPC and gRPC API, so scripts and AI agents can drive edits. · ★ 12,762

Existing Remotion and HyperFrames projects render as they are; it is in beta and installs from npm. · ★ 514

    AI VFX for filmmakers. Relight footage after the shoot by pulling normal, depth and other passes from ordinary video.
  • Boris FX Mocha Pro Paid
    Academy Award-winning planar tracking, roto and object removal plugin, with ML-based masking, for After Effects, Premiere, Resolve, Nuke and more.
