Refund policy

When a paid listing, sponsorship, Bot Store download, or paid report can be refunded, and how to ask.

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This page is the refund policy for payments you make to AgentScout. It is written in plain words. It is not legal advice. Card payments are taken by Stripe. You can also pay the same products with X Money to @clampdown19. The same rules apply either way.

The short version

  • The $15 fast-track listing is fully refunded if we decline the tool, or if it is not listed within 5 days. Once the listing is live, that fee is not refunded.
  • A sponsorship is fully refunded if you cancel before the slot starts. After it starts, the refund is prorated for the time left, or there is no refund if the placement has already run.
  • Bot Store packs are downloads. There is no refund after you download a pack, unless the pack is broken or not as described and you report that within 7 days.
  • The Directory Launch Pack is a paid add-on, not a download. You get a full refund if you ask before we start the submissions. After we start, the refund is prorated, or there is no refund once the work is delivered.
  • An MCP server test report, an AI visibility check, or the bundle of both is fully refunded if you ask before testing starts, or if we cannot run the test at all. Once the report is delivered, there is no refund, and you get one free retest or fix check of the same server or site within 30 days.

$15 fast-track listing

Fast-track is a one-time $15 fee to skip the standard review queue. You get a full refund if we decline the tool, or if the tool is not listed within 5 days of payment. Once the listing is live on AgentScout, the $15 fee is not refunded.

Sponsorships

Sponsorships are the paid placement slots, including the category pin and the weekly sitewide sponsor. You get a full refund if you cancel before the slot starts. After the slot starts, we refund the unused time (a prorated refund), or none of it if the placement has already run.

Bot Store packs

Bot Store packs and the flagship bots are downloads. There is no refund after you download the pack. If the pack is broken or not as described, email us within 7 days of the purchase and we will refund it.

Directory Launch Pack

The Directory Launch Pack is a separate paid add-on. We submit your tool to other directories. It is not a listing tier and it is not a download. You get a full refund if you ask before we start those submissions. After we start, the refund is prorated, or there is no refund once the work is delivered.

MCP server test reports and AI visibility checks

MCP Server Test Report is $99, or $79 intro. AI Visibility Check is $99, or $79 intro. Bundle: MCP server test and AI visibility check is $149, or $119 intro. Intro price for the first 10 reports or until December 31, 2026, whichever comes first.

  • You get a full refund if you ask before testing starts.
  • You get a full refund if we cannot run the test at all, for example when the MCP server or site cannot be reached and you cannot fix access.
  • Once a report is delivered, there is no refund. You get one free retest or fix check of the same server or site within 30 days.

Email support@agentscouthq.com. The offers are on the MCP server test report page and the AI visibility check page.

EU and UK buyers

By the pay buttons, you agree that delivery starts right away. If you are in the EU or the UK, that agreement ends the 14-day right to cancel. The refund rules above still apply when we decline a listing, miss the 5-day window, have not started a slot, or a download is broken or not as described.

How to ask for a refund

Email support@agentscouthq.com. Include the product, the date you paid, and the email address on the receipt. We reply to that address.
