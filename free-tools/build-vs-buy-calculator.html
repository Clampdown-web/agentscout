FAQ

How is the comparison calculated?

Buy = one-time setup + (seats × seat price + usage) × months. Build = setup hours × hourly cost + (hosting + model API + maintenance hours × hourly cost) × months. Break-even is the first month where building has cost no more in total than buying.

What does this leave out?

Things that are hard to price: security reviews, uptime, support, features the hosted tool adds over time, and what your team could build instead. Add rough figures to maintenance hours if they matter.

Are any prices filled in for me?

No. The starting numbers are examples only. Enter your real quotes and costs.

Is anything sent to a server?

No. The calculator runs in your browser.
