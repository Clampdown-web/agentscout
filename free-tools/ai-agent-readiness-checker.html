AI agent readiness checker

Enter any website and see how ready it is for AI agents and AI search: llms.txt, AI crawler rules in robots.txt, sitemap, structured data, and MCP or agent files, with a score and fixes.

Free, no sign-up. Runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type is sent to AgentScout or any AI provider.

llms.txt (a Markdown map of your site for AI models; make one with the llms.txt generator), robots.txt rules for AI crawlers such as GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and Google-Extended, a sitemap, structured data (JSON-LD), page title, description and Open Graph tags, readable text on the homepage, and machine-readable agent files: /.well-known/mcp.json, /.well-known/agent.json (A2A agent card), ai-plugin.json and openapi.json.

FAQ

What is AI agent readiness?

How easily AI agents, assistants and AI search engines can find, read and use your site: whether their crawlers are allowed, whether there's an llms.txt map, a sitemap, structured data and machine-readable agent files.

How is the score worked out?

Each check has a weight (llms.txt and AI crawler access count most). The score is the share of weights your site passes. It's a quick guide, not an official standard.

Is anything stored?

No. We fetch only public files from the site you enter and keep the result in a short cache for 10 minutes. You can check up to 10 sites a minute.

Why does my site say it can't be reached?

Some sites block automated requests or take longer than 6 seconds to answer. Only public web addresses can be checked.
